# Workforce Performance & Labor Cost Analysis

An end-to-end workforce analytics project that combines Python, MySQL, SQL, and Power BI to analyze employee performance, workforce productivity, labor costs, overtime, training expenses, and potential cost risks.

## Project Overview

Organizations need to balance employee performance with workforce costs. High overtime, increasing training expenses, and low employee performance can create significant operational and financial risks.

This project analyzes workforce data to identify performance trends, labor cost patterns, overtime usage, and employees who may represent potential cost risks.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow:

**Python → CSV Data → MySQL → SQL Analysis → Power BI → Business Insights**

---

## Business Objectives

- Analyze employee performance and productivity
- Measure total sales revenue generated by employees
- Calculate total labor costs
- Analyze overtime hours and overtime costs
- Evaluate training hours and training costs
- Compare employee performance with labor costs
- Identify potential cost-risk employees
- Provide interactive business insights through Power BI

--Potential Cost Risk Logic
Employees are classified as Potential Cost Risk when all of the following conditions are met:

Average Performance < 60
AND
Total Overtime Hours > 200
AND
Total Revenue < Total Labor Cost
